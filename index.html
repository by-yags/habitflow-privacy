This Privacy Policy explains how HabitFlow ByYags — Habit Planner (Android package com.byyags.habitflow; "HabitFlow," "the app"), developed by ByYags, handles information when you use the app.

In short: your habits, tasks, calendar events and reminders stay on your device. There is no HabitFlow account, server or cloud sync, and ByYags cannot see your data. The free app shows banner ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information as described in Section 4.

1. Information you enter

HabitFlow lets you create habits and record your progress (completed, partial and skipped days), tasks with due dates and notes, calendar events with times, locations and notes, categories, reminder settings and app preferences.

This information is stored in a private database on your device. It is not sent to ByYags or to anyone else, and HabitFlow does not require you to create an account. Android's automatic cloud backup of the app's data is disabled.

2. Reminders and notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by Android using the notification permission you grant. Notification text is created on your device and is not sent to any server. In Settings you can hide reminder details on the lock screen or use generic notification text that never shows the names of your habits, tasks or events.

3. Backups you create

When you use Export backup, HabitFlow writes a file to a location you choose with the Android file picker. Backup files are not encrypted: anyone who has the file can read its contents, so store it somewhere private. HabitFlow never uploads backups. Restore backup reads only the file you select.

4. Advertising and information handled by Google

HabitFlow displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google information such as your device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and advertisements, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses this information for purposes that may include ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

On supported Android versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs (such as Topics and Attribution Reporting), which you can manage in your device's Android ad privacy settings.

HabitFlow does not give the advertising SDK your habits, tasks, notes, events or any other content you enter.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), HabitFlow asks for your choices through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can review or change them at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. How information is used

  • To track your habits, plan tasks, show your calendar and calculate streaks and statistics, entirely on your device.
  • To schedule the reminders you set up.
  • To provide and measure advertisements, as described in Section 4.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set up. Requested only after an explanation, when you first turn on a reminder.
  • Run at startup: to restore scheduled reminders after your device restarts.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: used only by the advertising SDK.

HabitFlow does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, location, microphone, camera or device calendar.

7. Sharing

ByYags does not sell your information and does not receive the content you enter in HabitFlow. If ads are shown, Google receives the information described in Section 4 through its advertising SDK.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Use Settings → Delete all data, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app to remove it. Backup files you exported are managed by you. Data collected by Google through AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and your applicable controls.

9. Security

HabitFlow keeps your information in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The security of that information also depends on your device's lock screen and settings. Exported backups are unencrypted, so keep them in a safe place. Communications from the advertising SDK are managed by Google and protected in transit.

10. Children

HabitFlow is a general-purpose productivity app for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and ByYags does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

ByYags may update this policy when the app's features, advertising integrations or applicable requirements change. An updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
